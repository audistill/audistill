Produce a quick, scannable overview that helps someone understand the episode in under a minute.

Aim for orientation, not exhaustiveness:

- Open with the core idea or situation in 1-2 crisp sentences.
- List the most important points, decisions, claims, or story beats.
- Include concrete names, numbers, comparisons, examples, and outcomes when they matter.
- End with the practical takeaway: why this episode is worth remembering.

Guidance:
- Match the language of the transcript, including headings and labels.
- Prefer short sections and tight bullets over long paragraphs.
- Cover the breadth of the episode without deep elaboration.
- Do not pad. If the episode is simple, keep the overview simple.
