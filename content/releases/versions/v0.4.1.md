## Added

### In-app Help and What's New

Audistill now bundles Help articles and Release Notes inside the app. The sidebar Help button opens local guidance, and update banners open bundled What's New notes instead of sending you to the Git repository.

## Improved

### Clearer in-app Help guidance

Help articles now give friendlier onboarding guidance, correct the distinction between Audistill's built-in local Transcription Model and the OpenRouter Models used for Recipes and Chat, and include a direct support contact.
