## Improved

### Audistill 1.0 is free and open source

Audistill 1.0 is here, and it is now free. There is no Trial, License, or Activation any more, and every feature is available in the signed app. If your Trial had ended or your License could not be verified, everything works again after this update. Your Library and settings are untouched.

You still bring your own OpenRouter API key and pay OpenRouter separately for Model usage. If you would like to support the project, Settings has an optional donation link. Donating is never required and unlocks nothing.
