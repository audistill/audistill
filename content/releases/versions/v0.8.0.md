## Added

### Export a Tab as PDF

The Tab export menu now offers editable Markdown or a formatted PDF. PDF files use a clean light layout, include the Tab and Episode names, and preserve rich content for sharing or printing.

### Feeds check themselves for new episodes

Audistill now checks your feed subscriptions shortly after launch and periodically while it is open, so opening the app is enough to see what has arrived. You can also check a single feed from its header, or every feed at once from the Feeds section.

New items are counted on the feed's sidebar row and totalled on the Feeds header, and each one carries an accent dot in the feed view. Opening a Feed leaves the count intact until you choose **Clear new**. Checking only fetches feed metadata — nothing is downloaded, transcribed, or sent to a Model until you choose an Episode, and it works the same on Trial or with a License.

A feed that cannot be reached shows a quiet marker on its row with the reason on hover, does not interrupt you, and is tried again on the next round.

### Subscribe to YouTube channels

Paste a YouTube channel, handle, playlist, or video URL to subscribe to its upload feed. New videos appear alongside podcast Feed Items and stay metadata-only until you choose to add one to your Library.

## Improved

### A calmer, more workable feed view

The Feed Workspace now sits in a centred reading column, and each row presents its title over one compact line of available metadata. Row-level Add controls appear on hover or keyboard focus instead of reserving permanent space.

Bulk selection is now an explicit mode. Choose **Select**, click any rows you want, then add them from the selection bar. Ordinary row activation no longer creates a hidden multi-selection: it focuses the Item and marks a New Item seen.

### A clearer Feed view

Feed items now sit in a readable column with duration in its own aligned lane, so a long feed can be scanned at a glance on any window size, and the Add action appears beside the item it adds rather than at the far edge of the window. Items already in your Library read as links to their Episode, everything else reads as selectable, and each item shows a short description excerpt where the feed provides one.

The list can now be driven entirely from the keyboard: arrow through items, hold Shift to extend a selection, and add the selection without reaching for the pointer.

### Clear feed action feedback

Feed actions now show progress and results where they happen. **Clear new** removes attention indicators without removing Items from All, offers a brief Undo action, and leaves Items that arrive afterward untouched. Failures stay visible in the Feed Workspace without interrupting you.

### Clearer Feed triage

Opening a Feed leaves New indicators intact until you act. Activating a New Item marks it seen and offers a brief Undo, while **Clear new** handles the whole current New snapshot. **New** is now a focused subset of **All**, so every New Item also appears in the complete catalogue.

## Fixed

### Adding YouTube videos no longer fails on large metadata

Adding a YouTube video from a Feed or URL no longer fails with a misleading “yt-dlp may be outdated” error when YouTube returns very large video metadata. If yt-dlp reports that no JavaScript runtime is available, Audistill now recommends installing Deno.

### Complete, stable feed catalogues

New Feed Items now appear in All immediately after manual and background checks. Long RSS catalogues are retained and loaded incrementally, while virtualized rows keep the workspace responsive. Background arrivals preserve your reading position and show a small “new items above” control when needed.

### Feed New badge stays in sync with the feed view

The sidebar's New count for a feed could drift out of step with what the feed view showed — a feed opened elsewhere, a background check, or clearing New from the sidebar context menu could leave stale New indicators in an already-open feed view, or vice versa. The feed view now always reflects the same New state as the sidebar badge.

### Feed selection highlight and row hover

Selecting an item in a feed showed a stray blue outline instead of Audistill's accent highlight, and rows gave no hover feedback at all. Selection now uses the same accent tint and ring as the rest of the app, and rows respond to the pointer. The same missing hover feedback has been restored across the Tab bars, Episode header, and Help view.

### More reliable YouTube channel subscriptions

YouTube Feed previews and refreshes now use the configured `yt-dlp` binary instead of depending on YouTube's unreliable Atom endpoint.
