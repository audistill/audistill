title: Record System Audio and Microphones in Audistill
---

Start a Recording Session from the Add menu, capture audio playing on your Mac, a selected built-in or external microphone, or both, and stop it to create a Recorded Episode. Audistill requests Screen Recording or Microphone access only when you enable the corresponding source. Live source meters reflect the actual System Audio and Microphone Source levels before and during capture. The pinned Recording Workspace stays active while you view other Episodes or hide Audistill, supports pause and resume without adding paused time to the Transcript, and helps recover from permission changes, disconnected microphones, Mac sleep, low storage, deliberate app quit choices, and interrupted sessions found on the next launch. Transcription stays on-device, and temporary captured audio is deleted after the Transcript is stored.
