Your **Library** is the home for every Episode in Audistill. New Episodes start in the **Inbox**, and you can organize them with Folders, Starred shortcuts, workspace tabs, and bulk actions.

## Inbox, Folders, and Starred

The Inbox is the landing zone for Episodes that are not in a Folder. It always exists and cannot be deleted.

Folders are your own containers for organizing Episodes. Use the + button near the Folders heading or the folder context menu to create them. Folders can be nested. If you delete a Folder, its Episodes move back to the Inbox.

Starred is a shortcut section. Starring an Episode does not move it out of its Folder or Inbox; it simply makes the Episode appear in Starred as well. Use Command-D to star or unstar the active Episode.

## Opening Episodes

Single-click an Episode to open it in a preview workspace tab. Opening another Episode can replace that preview. Double-click an Episode to pin it so it stays in the workspace tab bar until you close it.

Open workspace tabs are restored when Audistill restarts.

## Moving, renaming, and deleting

Right-click an Episode for common actions such as Rename, Move to, Export Episode as Markdown, Delete, Cancel, or Retry depending on the Episode status.

You can drag Episodes into Folders or back to the Inbox. Select multiple Episodes with Command-click or Shift-click, then use the action bar to move, delete, or export them together.

## Finding Episodes

The sidebar search filters Episodes by title. Chat can search more deeply across Transcripts and Tab content when you ask it to look across your Library.

The Inbox sort button cycles between Newest, Oldest, and Longest. Date dividers appear for date-based Inbox sorting.
