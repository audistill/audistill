Audistill keeps your Library viewable without an active **License**, but creating new content is gated after the **Trial** ends. Existing Episodes, Transcripts, Tabs, folders, and exports remain accessible.

## Trial

Official builds start with a 14-day Trial. During the Trial, Ingest, Chat, Recipe execution, and other creation workflows are unlocked.

The Trial state appears in the app chrome and in Settings → License.

## License and Activation

A License is a one-time purchase validated through Polar. **Activation** binds that License to your Mac.

Some License tiers allow multiple activations. If you reach the activation limit, open the customer portal from Settings → License and deactivate another machine before activating this one.

## What is gated

When your Trial expires and no valid License is active, Audistill blocks actions that create new Episodes or Model-generated content, including Ingest, Chat messages, Recipe execution, and Regenerate.

Reading existing Episodes, editing existing Tabs, organizing the Library, and exporting existing content remain available.

## If a License becomes invalid

If Audistill cannot verify your License, Settings → License explains the current state. Connect to the internet and restart, enter a new key, or use the customer portal to manage activations.
